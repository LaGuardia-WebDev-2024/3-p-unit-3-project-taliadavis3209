Recipe #1

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 36
Ingredients Quantity
butter, softened 🧈 ¾ cup
packed brown sugar 🍬 1 ¼ cups
white sugar 🍬 ¼ cup
vanilla extract 🫙 1 teaspoon
eggs 🥚 1
gluten-free baking mix 🍪 2 ¼ cups
baking soda 🫙 1 teaspoon
baking powder 🫙 1 teaspoon
salt 🧂 1 teaspoon/td>
semisweet chocolate chips 🍫 12 ounces

Step 1: Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F ( 190 degrees C).
Step 2: Grease baking sheets.
Step 3:Beat butter and sugar together in a medium bowl until lighter in color.
Step 4:Gradually beat in egg substitute and vanilla.
Step 5:Sift gluten- free flour mix, baking soda, baking powder, and salt together; stir into butter mixture until blended.
Step 6:Finally, stir in the chocolate chips. Drop spoonfuls of cookie dough 2 inches apart on prepared baking sheets. Bake in preheated oven until edges are golden brown, about 6 to 8 minutes. Let cookies cool on baking sheet for a few minutes before removing to wire racks.

Recipe #2

  • Time: _
  • Serves: _
Ingredients Quantity Icing
butter, softened 🧈 ¾ cup ¼ cup cream cheese, softened
white sugar 🍬 1 cup ¼ cup unsalted butter, softened
vanilla extract 🫙 1 teaspoon 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
eggs 🥚 2 1 ½ cups confectioners' sugar
gluten-free flour 🍪 2 ½ cups
baking soda 🫙 1 teaspoon
salt 🧂 ½ teaspoon/td>

Step 1: Gather all ingredients.
Step 2:Make the cookies: Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl. Beat white sugar, butter, and vanilla in a bowl with an electric mixer until creamy, 3 to 4 minutes.
Step 3 :Beat in eggs, 1 at a time, until mixture is smooth.
Step 4:Gradually mix in flour mixture until a dough forms.
Step 5:Form dough into a ball. Wrap ball with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 1 hour.
Step 6:Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C).
Step 7:Line two baking sheets with parchment paper or silicon baking mats.
Step 8:Roll dough on a lightly floured surface and cut into shapes using desired cookie cutters.
Step 9:Arrange cookies on the prepared baking sheets.
Step 10:Bake in the preheated oven until edges begin to brown, about 10 minutes, switching racks halfway through.
Step 11:Cool on the baking sheet briefly before removing to a wire rack to cool, 8 to 10 minutes more.
Step 12: While the cookies are cooling, make the icing:
Step 13:Beat cream cheese, butter, and vanilla in a bowl using an electric mixer until well combined.
Step 14:Slowly beat in confectioners' sugar until icing is smooth.
Step 15:Spread icing onto cooled cookies.
